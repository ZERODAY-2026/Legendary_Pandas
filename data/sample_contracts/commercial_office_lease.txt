COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. BASIC LEASE PROVISIONS
Date of Lease: March 15, 2026
Landlord: Metro Commercial Holdings Inc.
Tenant: Nova Apex Technologies LLC
Premises: Suite 400, 100 Financial Plaza, Metropolis

2. TERM AND COMMENCEMENT
The Lease Term shall be for a duration of twenty-four (24) months, commencing on May 1, 2026, and expiring on April 30, 2028.

3. BASE RENT
Tenant covenants and agrees to pay to Landlord monthly Base Rent in the amount of $3,500.00, payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month. Payments received after the fifth (5th) business day shall incur a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the unpaid balance.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Concurrently with the execution hereof, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $5,000.00. The deposit shall secure faithful performance by Tenant of all covenants and obligations. Landlord may apply all or part of the deposit to remedy any monetary default.

5. USE OF PREMISES
The Premises shall be utilized exclusively for general office use, software engineering, and administrative operations, and for no other purpose without prior written approval.

6. ALTERATIONS
Tenant shall not perform any structural alterations, wiring modifications, or interior renovations without Landlord's prior written approval. All approved alterations shall become the property of Landlord upon lease expiry unless stipulated otherwise.

7. DEFAULT AND TERMINATION
If Tenant defaults in the payment of rent or compliance with any covenant herein, Landlord shall provide written notice specifying the default. Tenant shall have ten (10) calendar days from receipt of notice to cure a monetary default, and thirty (30) days for a non-monetary default. Failure to cure shall entitle Landlord to terminate the lease and re-enter the Premises.

Landlord: Metro Commercial Holdings Inc.
Tenant: Nova Apex Technologies LLC
