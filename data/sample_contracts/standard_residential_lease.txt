RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES
This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made this 1st day of January, 2026, by and between Oakridge Properties LLC ("Landlord"), and Jane Doe ("Tenant").

2. PREMISES
Landlord leases to Tenant the real property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Apartment 3B, Springfield ("Premises").

3. TERM
The term of this lease shall commence on February 1, 2026, and shall continue as a fixed-term lease expiring on January 31, 2027.

4. RENT
Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent in the amount of $1,200.00, payable on or before the 1st day of each calendar month. If rent is not received by Landlord by 11:59 PM on the 5th day of the month, a late fee of $50.00 shall be assessed.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $1,500.00 as a Security Deposit. The Security Deposit shall be held in an escrow account. The deposit shall be returned to Tenant within 21 days following lease termination, less any lawful deductions for physical damages beyond normal wear and tear.

6. UTILITIES
Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all electricity, gas, internet, and cable services. Landlord shall be responsible for water, sewer, and municipal trash collection.

7. PETS
No animals, livestock, or pets of any kind (including dogs, cats, birds, and reptiles) shall be brought upon or kept in or about the Premises without the express prior written consent of Landlord.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant agrees to maintain the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord in writing of any defective condition, water leak, or plumbing malfunction. Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs and heating/cooling maintenance.

9. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT
Tenant shall not assign this lease or sublet any portion of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Any unauthorized subletting shall be deemed a material breach and grounds for immediate lease termination.

10. TERMINATION AND NOTICE
Either party may terminate this tenancy upon its stated expiration date by providing at least 30 days prior written notice to the other party. In the event of material non-compliance or non-payment of rent, Landlord may issue a 3-day notice to quit as permitted by local law.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.

Landlord: Oakridge Properties LLC
Tenant: Jane Doe
